# v273 behavior

- Team Intelligence cards now show the next opponent's current table position, venue PPG and kickoff time.
- Board, Bankers, Full Engine Board and individual Engine cards show both teams' position/PPG plus kickoff.
- Clicking a team/context opens a public Team Snapshot with Last 5 form, position, PPG, O/U 1.5/2.5/3.5, GG/NG and Fail-to-Score.
- Exact Last-5 goal/BTTS figures are calculated only from settled fixtures actually present in the loaded dataset. Missing history is shown as unavailable rather than estimated.
- No private engine weights, route rules or learning policy are exposed.
